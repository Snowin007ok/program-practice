Problems we solve

The daily issues that quietly cost a farm money

Most traditional sheds are run on experience and constant attention. That works — until something slips at 2am. These are the six problems farm owners raise with us most often.

  • Temperature swings

    Day-to-night shifts and seasonal heat stress affect bird comfort, feed intake and flock uniformity before anyone notices on the floor.

  • High feed wastage

    Manual feeding means spillage, uneven distribution and stock that is hard to account for — on your single largest running cost.

  • Inconsistent water supply

    Blocked nipples, pressure drops and dry lines can run unnoticed for hours, with a direct effect on intake and bird health.

  • Dependence on labour

    Routine tasks tie the farm to staff availability. Skilled hands are scarce, and a missed round becomes a production issue.

  • Problems found too late

    A fan that stopped in the night or a feed line that jammed is usually discovered on the next round — hours after it mattered.

  • Little farm visibility

    Without records, comparing sheds, batches or sites is guesswork — so it is hard to prove what actually improved.

Take one system or the full package. Everything is specified after we have walked your sheds, so it suits your bird type, capacity and budget.

    Sensor-driven fans, foggers and curtains that hold your target temperature and humidity band automatically, and log every change.

    One dashboard for every shed, with alerts to your phone the moment a reading moves outside the range you set.

    Conditions are held to a set band automatically, day and night, instead of being corrected by hand after they drift.
